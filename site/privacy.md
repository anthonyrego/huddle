# Privacy policy

<p class="date">Effective [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]</p>

Huddle is a meeting app operated by [[OPERATOR]] ("we"). This policy says what
Huddle collects, why, who else handles it, how long it's kept, and what you can
do about it.

## What we collect

- **Your phone number.** You sign in with it: we text you a code. If someone
  invites you to a meeting by your number, we keep it with that invitation.
- **Your name**, as you give it, which others in your meetings see.
- **Your meetings:** which meetings you create, are invited to and join, and when.
- **What's said in a meeting.** Meeting audio is sent to the AI whiteboard,
  which transcribes it so it can follow what's said and draw when asked. We keep
  the transcript and every version of the meeting's whiteboard so the meeting can
  be looked back on. When a meeting is recorded, everyone is told as they join,
  and its audio is kept with the transcript.
- **Camera and screen share video** passes through our media server to the
  others in the meeting. We don't record it.
- **Technical logs:** connection times, errors and the app's version, to keep
  the service running.

## How we use it

To sign you in, run your meetings, deliver invitations and reminders, run the
AI whiteboard, and keep the service working and secure. We don't sell personal
information, and we don't use it for advertising.

## Who else handles it

These services handle data for us, only to provide Huddle:

- **Twilio** sends sign-in codes, invitations and reminders by text message. It
  gets your phone number and the message.
- **Google (the Gemini API)** transcribes meeting audio and powers the AI
  whiteboard. It gets meeting audio and text while the meeting runs.
- **Our hosting provider** runs our servers, database and storage.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

**Text messaging:** no mobile information will be shared with third parties or
affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging opt-in data and
consent are not shared with any third parties.

## How long we keep it

- Your account, until you delete it.
- Meeting transcripts, whiteboard history and recordings: [[RETENTION]].
- Technical logs: up to 30 days.

## Your choices

- Delete your account in the app, or ask us at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]]. We delete
  your data within 30 days, except what the law makes us keep.
- Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].
- Stop text messages by replying **STOP** (see [Text messages](sms)).

## Children

Huddle isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app
when the change is significant.
