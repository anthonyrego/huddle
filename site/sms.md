# Text messages

<p class="date">Effective [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]</p>

Huddle, operated by [[OPERATOR]], sends text messages about meetings.

## What we send

- **Sign-in codes**, when you sign in to Huddle with your phone number.
- **Meeting invitations**, when someone invites your number to a Huddle meeting.
  Each says who invited you, the meeting's name and time, and how to join.
- **Meeting reminders**, shortly before a meeting you were invited to.

We never send marketing.

## How you agree to them

- **When you sign in,** you ask for the code, and you agree to receive
  invitations and reminders for meetings you're invited to.
- **When someone invites you** and you don't use Huddle yet, the person inviting
  you confirms that you've agreed to receive a text invitation from Huddle. The
  invitation says who sent it, and every message tells you how to stop.

## How often

It depends on how many meetings you're invited to: usually one invitation and
one reminder per meeting, plus a code each time you sign in.

## Stopping and help

- Reply **STOP** to any message to stop all texts from Huddle. We'll confirm,
  and send nothing more unless you reply **START**.
- Reply **HELP** for help, or write to [[CONTACT_EMAIL]].

Message and data rates may apply. Carriers aren't liable for delayed or
undelivered messages.

See the [privacy policy](privacy) for how we handle your phone number.
